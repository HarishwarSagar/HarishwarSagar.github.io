The throughline

From DevOpsto revenue

I started in engineering — building, shipping and operating systems at scale. At Oracle as a DevOps Engineer, and before that as a Software Engineer at Checkpoint Systems, I learned how reliable systems are designed: instrumented, automated, and accountable to real numbers.

Then I moved closer to the money. I took ownership of revenue — not just the tooling around it, but the strategy, the pricing, the pipeline and the P&L. I ran the full GTM motion across paid media, SEO, marketplaces and conversion funnels, and watched what actually moved the number.

That combination is the whole point. Most revenue leaders can read a dashboard; few can build the dashboard, wire the automation behind it, and then make the commercial call. I do both.

My philosophy is a Revenue OS: treat go-to-market like a system. Every lead source, every pricing rule, every forecast and every manual task is a component that can be measured, automated and improved — so the business runs on signal, not guesswork, and growth compounds without the headcount.
